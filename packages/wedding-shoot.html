Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking:

When do we receive our wedding photos?

You receive a priority 48-hour teaser set of 15–20 photos for immediate social sharing. Full retouched galleries are delivered within 3 weeks, and printed photobooks within 3–4 weeks.

Do you travel outside Kakamega & Nairobi?

Yes! Our wedding crew travels across all 47 counties in Kenya and internationally. Travel logistics are arranged transparently with no hidden fees.

How does the wedding deposit and booking work?

A 40% booking deposit secures your exclusive date on our calendar. The remaining balance is comfortably settled upon delivery of your full retouched gallery and films.
