Maternity Shoot FAQs

Comfort and planning guide for expectant mothers:

When is the best week for a maternity photoshoot?

We recommend booking between 28 and 34 weeks. Your bump will be beautifully rounded, and you will still feel comfortable moving and posing without fatigue.

Do you provide the silk fabrics and drapes?

Yes! We have an exquisite collection of luxury silk wraps in champagne gold, emerald green, midnight black, and ivory white, plus specialized wind fans for dramatic sculptural draping.

Can my partner and children be in the photos?

Yes! Under our Gold and Platinum packages, your partner and older children are warmly welcomed to join in the portraits at zero extra fee.

What undergarments should I wear under silk wraps?

Seamless, skin-tone/nude strapless underwear and bras work best for smooth drape lines under silk and sheer fabrics.
