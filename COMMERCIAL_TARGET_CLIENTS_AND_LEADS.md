## 📌 Executive Strategy: Why We Shifted From Freelancers to Commercial Enterprises

| Attribute | Solo Freelancer / Portrait Photographer | Commercial Enterprise (Rental, AV, Production, Decor) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Bottleneck** | Lack of leads / empty calendar | Wasting 15+ hours/week manually typing itemized quotes |
| **Quotation Complexity** | Simple (1–3 standard packages) | Complex (20–100+ inventory items, add-ons, days, crew) |
| **Willingness to Pay** | Very low (prefers free Canva/WhatsApp) | High (software saves labor costs and prevents booking errors) |
| **Contract / Deal Size** | Ksh 1,500 – Ksh 25,000 | Ksh 50,000 – Ksh 2,500,000+ |
| **Fraud & Verification Need** | Minimal / Almost none | Critical (deposit verification, equipment theft, fake quotes) |

## 🗺️ Google Maps Prospecting Guide

When searching for these brands on Google Maps:
1. Open [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com).
2. Enter the **Google Maps Search Keyword** provided for each sector.
3. Tap on the business listing to grab:
   - **Office Address / Location**
   - **Direct Business WhatsApp / Mobile Number**
   - **Official Website URL**
   - **Reviews / Operating Hours**
4. Check if they have an active website. If their current website lacks an instant quote builder or is outdated, **they are an immediate warm prospect**.

## 🎯 The 3 Golden Rules When Pitching These Enterprises
1. **Never pitch the photographer, pitch the Operations / Sales Manager:** The owner or operations head is the one suffering from slow quotes, lost inventory, and manual work.
2. **Focus on Time & Money Saved:** Tell them: *"This replaces 2 hours of typing Excel sheets every day with a 15-second customer self-service quote."*
3. **Show, Don't Explain:** Drop a 30-second screen-recording video of the quotation workstation in action. When they see the deliverables calculate and the verified QR stamp appear, they immediately understand the power.
