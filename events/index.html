Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an event date with Laureighn Events?
Tap "Book on WhatsApp" or use our interactive booking form below. Provide your planned event date, event type (wedding, ruracio, birthday, graduation, funeral, or corporate), and venue. A deposit locks in your date and crew on our production calendar.
Do you travel outside Kakamega and Nairobi?
Yes, absolutely! We travel across all 47 counties of Kenya including Kisumu, Eldoret, Nakuru, Naivasha, Mombasa, Machakos, Nyeri, and rural homesteads for traditional ceremonies and burials. Travel logistics are calculated transparently.
How do you handle traditional ruracio and koito negotiations?
Our crews understand cultural etiquette across Luhya, Kikuyu, Kalenjin, Luo, Kisii, and other Kenyan traditions. We work quietly during delicate negotiation moments and capture elder blessings with wireless lapel microphones for clear audio.
How fast do we receive our photos and video reels?
We deliver a priority 48-hour sneak peek for WhatsApp and social media (30–50 photos + short teaser). Full edited high-resolution galleries and highlight videos are delivered in 2–3 weeks for weddings or 3–5 days for corporate summits and parties.
Can you provide live streaming for relatives abroad / in the diaspora?
Yes! We offer professional multi-camera live streaming setups for YouTube, Zoom, or Facebook with clean audio feeds, allowing relatives anywhere in the world to join the celebration or memorial service in real-time.
