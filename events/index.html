FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a corporate summit or secure our event date?
Tap "Book on WhatsApp" or use our interactive booking form below. Provide your planned date, event type, and venue location. A 50% deposit locks in your date and crew.
Where are your production crews based?
We have dedicated production hubs in Kakamega and Nairobi. We travel seamlessly across all 47 counties of Kenya for summits, conferences, and celebrations.
Do you travel for events outside Kakamega & Nairobi?
Yes! We regularly document corporate summits, galas, and celebrations in Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Naivasha, Mombasa, and beyond. Travel quotes are shared transparently upon inquiry.
How and when do I receive my photos and videos?
We share a 48-hour sneak peek for social media and press releases right after event day. Full retouched online galleries and cinematic video cuts are delivered via a private high-resolution cloud link within 2 to 3 days.
Can we customize our event production package?
Absolutely. Every event has unique logistics. We frequently build custom coverage combinations including 4K drone cinematography, multi-camera crews, and extra photobooks.
