✦ Event Booking & Production Standards

Date Reservation

A 50% deposit secures your event date on our master production calendar. Your date and crew are locked once confirmed, with the balance due on or before event day.

High-Resolution Delivery

Every event package includes all colour-graded, high-resolution master photographs. No watermarks. Full printing and personal rights included.

All weddings and major galas receive a 48-hour sneak peek gallery of 30–50 highlight photos for immediate social sharing and media press releases.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a wedding or secure our event date?
Tap "Book on WhatsApp" or use our interactive booking form below. Provide your planned date, event type, and venue location. A 50% deposit locks in your date and crew.
Where are your production crews based?
We have dedicated production hubs in Kakamega and Nairobi. We travel seamlessly across all 47 counties of Kenya for weddings, traditional ceremonies, and summits.
Do you travel for destination weddings outside Kakamega & Nairobi?
Yes! We regularly document weddings, ruracio ceremonies, and summits in Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret, Naivasha, Mombasa, and beyond. Travel quotes are shared transparently upon inquiry.
How and when do I receive my photos and cinema film?
We share a 48-hour sneak peek for social media and family sharing right after event day. Full retouched online galleries and cinematic video cuts are delivered via a private high-resolution cloud link within 2 to 3 days of your photo selection.
Can we customize our wedding or corporate summit package?
Absolutely. Every event has unique logistics. We frequently build custom coverage combinations including 4K drone cinematography, live streaming, after-party crews, and extra photobooks.
